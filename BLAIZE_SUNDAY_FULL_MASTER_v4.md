## 1. Executive System Definition

BLAIZE SUNDAY is not an anonymous virtual character, a novelty-rap concept, or a collection of disconnected AI outputs. He is a **human-fronted, genre-stable artist identity** whose music, voice, visuals, release strategy, and audience interactions all express controlled contradiction. His immaculate surface repeatedly collides with mundane details, emotional instability, digital interference, and self-aware humor.

The operating system converts that identity into a disciplined creative and commercial loop. It starts with a locked persona and voice, produces records through repeatable quality gates, turns each approved song into a complete content package, plans and prepares distribution, measures real audience response, and feeds validated learning into the next release. The system can later be instantiated for additional artists without allowing their creative identities, voices, data, or rights records to blur together.

| System layer | Primary job | Non-negotiable outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Canon** | Define identity, voice, sonic language, visual world, and boundaries. | Every output feels like BLAIZE within seconds. |
| **Creative engine** | Generate, evaluate, revise, and finish lyrics, music, vocals, audio, and visuals. | Quality over generation volume. |
| **Asset and provenance system** | Version, tag, store, approve, and trace every asset. | No lost master, unclear source, or “mostly done” state. |
| **Release engine** | Package, schedule, distribute, and launch approved music and media. | No release proceeds without complete audio, metadata, rights, and content. |
| **Audience engine** | Publish content, engage respectfully, learn from response, and build owned audience. | Recognition precedes monetization. |
| **Portfolio operating layer** | Reuse shared infrastructure while isolating each artist’s canon and rights. | Scale artists without homogenizing them. |

## 2. Operating Doctrine and Strategic Constraints

The system must remain **artist-first**. Its immediate objective is not a twelve-track album, custom app, autonomous posting machine, or maximum output volume. It is one locked core voice, three undeniable singles, one coherent visual world, and enough consistent repetition for audiences to recognize BLAIZE before they are told his name.

The approved proof cycle is therefore:

1. **LOOKS EXPENSIVE** — the manifesto: cool plus vulnerable.
2. **MY THERAPIST BLOCKED ME** — the discovery record: funny plus vulnerable.
3. **BAD DECISIONS, GREAT OUTFIT** — the movement record: cool plus funny.

**PRETTY BOY PROBLEMS** becomes the fourth single or the first expansion track only after the initial proof cycle produces evidence of repeat interest. The broader Era One track architecture remains a development map, not an automatic production queue.

| Required discipline | What it means in practice | What must not happen |
|---|---|---|
| **Recognition before explanation** | Start with visual and sonic fragments that communicate presence. | Exposition-heavy “character launch” content. |
| **One artist, one core voice** | Four delivery modes may shade a single identity. | Provider-default voices or unrelated voices per song. |
| **Proof before scale** | Use the three-single cycle to test voice, hooks, visuals, and response. | Rendering a twelve-track album before market signal. |
| **Continuity before spectacle** | Prove face, motion, styling, and lip-sync continuity before a full video. | Isolated visual generations that look like unrelated experiments. |
| **Complete package before release** | Audio, metadata, provenance, rights, artwork, and content bank must be ready. | Calling rough drafts finished or generating in panic after launch. |
| **Human accountability** | Humans approve artist-defining, legal, financial, and public-facing actions. | Unreviewed distribution submissions, rights claims, paid spend, or sensitive fan replies. |

# Part I — BLAIZE SUNDAY Canon

## 3. Persona Bible

### 3.1 Core Positioning

> **BLAIZE SUNDAY is the beautifully overprepared guy whose outfit says success while his internal operating system throws errors.**

He is luxury-coded but never shallow, emotionally exposed but never fragile, funny but never cartoonish, and self-aware without becoming detached. He performs confidence while admitting, through glitches in the performance, that certainty has failed him. He is queer by nature of the world and relationships he inhabits, never as a campaign mechanic or forced disclosure narrative.

| Persona dimension | Canonical expression | Creative implication |
|---|---|---|
| **Surface** | Clean styling, access, confident presence, internet-native charisma. | Use polished framing, tailored silhouettes, and composed body language. |
| **Behavior** | Deadpan wit, controlled emotional leakage, awareness of his own performance. | Put the joke near the self; let sincerity interrupt the pose. |
| **Writing engine** | Contradiction, short quotable fragments, emotional disruption. | Every record moves from flex to puncture to reflection. |
| **Core truth** | Identity is performance under pressure; luxury distorts emotion; intimacy is partially misread. | Avoid generic wealth fantasy and generic sadness. |
| **Balance** | 35% funny, 35% cool, 30% vulnerable. | A release slate should prove all three dimensions. |
| **Commercial objective** | Build recognizable artist IP with repeatable songs and content. | Make every release a coherent unit of music, imagery, lore, and audience memory. |

### 3.2 Persona Boundaries

BLAIZE may be glamorous, flirtatious, emotionally evasive, vain, observant, and occasionally chaotic. However, the persona cannot become cruel, misogynistic, casually contemptuous, identity-exploitative, or a soundalike of another performer. Humor should generally implicate BLAIZE himself rather than diminish outsiders. Luxury is conveyed through texture, service rituals, silhouette, light, glass, chrome, tailoring, access, and environments—not lists of designer labels.

| Approved territory | Avoided territory |
|---|---|
| Specific admissions hidden inside behavior. | Announcing emotions without a concrete scene or action. |
| Attraction, romantic ambiguity, jealousy, and queer intimacy expressed naturally. | Rainbow-washed launch campaigns or obligatory identity exposition. |
| Glamour disrupted by mundane systems, bad timing, and operational failures. | Pure consumer flexing or generic “money fixes everything” fantasy. |
| Deadpan self-knowledge and purposeful contradiction. | Punch-down jokes, caricature, or endless irony with no emotional cost. |
| Intimacy, confidence, and sexual tone that feels assured. | Performative seduction with no character or consequence. |

### 3.3 Era One: *LOOKS EXPENSIVE, FEELS WEIRD*

Era One presents an immaculate public facade corrupted by uncertainty, mundane systems, mistaken access, and fragments of alternate outcomes called **STATIC**. The core colors are obsidian black, bone and ivory, liquid amber, chrome, and bruised violet. This is **dirty city × immaculate styling**: formal silhouettes in convenience spaces, white fur in banal locations, tailored clothes in exhausted environments, and polished rooms with one visibly wrong detail.

The visual language has a single governing law:

> **Every image must contain one beautiful thing and one wrong thing.**

## 4. Voice Bible

### 4.1 Target Canonical Voice Specification

The target canonical BLAIZE vocal identity is **SUNDAY AFTER MIDNIGHT**: a warm low-mid masculine voice with lightly textured resonance, close-mic intimacy, lazy phrasing slightly behind the beat, controlled emotional leakage, and occasional vocal fry at phrase endings. It should communicate: *“I am not trying, but I am precise.”*

This is a single voice identity, not four unrelated speakers. It is expressed through four authorized performance modes.

| Mode | Vocal behavior | Approved use | Production treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SUNDAY TALK** | Close, dry, conversational, almost spoken. | Deadpan observations, flirtation, intimate setup lines. | Minimal tuning; narrow, intimate foreground. |
| **BLAIZE MODE** | Rhythm-forward, sharper consonants, more attack, compact phrasing. | Flexes, pace changes, punchlines, pre-chorus pressure. | Controlled compression and selective doubles. |
| **VELVET** | Smooth melodic extension, airy doubles, restrained falsetto. | Hooks, romance, longing, lift. | Wide but soft doubles; avoid glossy anonymity. |
| **ZERO STATIC** | Nearly exposed voice, reduced ad-libs, minimal theatricality. | Bridges and moments where the pose breaks. | Light processing; no masking emotional detail. |

This section defines the target specification. It does not represent a completed or locked voice asset until the Part V G2 protocol passes and the Artist Principal approves the exact version.

### 4.2 Voice Selection and Lock Procedure

No production scale proceeds until a single core voice is chosen. Three eligible timbral candidates should perform exactly the same 25–35 second audition script in each required mode, then be evaluated under the Part V blind-test protocol. Candidate names are **Glass Baritone**, **Chrome Tenor**, and **Velvet Burn**. The final candidate must be masculine, contemporary, naturally expressive, and distinct without resembling a living performer.

**Audition script**

> Card declined, fit approved.  
> I look certain, feel confused.  
> Bad decisions, great outfit.  
> I got good at looking certain  
> long before I felt okay.

| Criterion | Weight | Passing interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| Recognizable identity | 30% | Listeners can identify a distinct performer rather than a generic AI vocal. |
| Naturalness | 25% | The delivery sounds intentional and emotionally plausible. |
| Emotional range | 20% | The voice can credibly move from deadpan to intimate exposure. |
| Hook lift | 15% | The voice can elevate and hold a melodic hook. |
| Pronunciation and consistency | 10% | Clear diction, stable timbre, and repeatable delivery. |

The same-artist recognition test is then run across the four modes. The voice is locked only when at least **70%** of blind evaluators recognize one performer across the set. The locked voice is stored as a versioned, approved identity asset. It may only be changed through a documented canon revision approved by the Artist Principal and Creative Director.

## 5. Lyric and Narrative Bible

### 5.1 The BLAIZE Lyric Equation

Every BLAIZE song contains the following core devices:

1. **Visual flex:** status, access, beauty, control, or desire.
2. **Mundane operational detail:** a receipt, card reader, Wi-Fi, screen, hotel key, customer-service hold, or other ordinary system.
3. **Self-implicating puncture:** humor, doubt, or contradiction that destabilizes the flex.
4. **Emotional leak:** a specific admission revealed through behavior rather than a generic declaration.
5. **Caption line:** a compact phrase that stands alone in a screenshot or post.
6. **Scene-producing image:** a line that can immediately become a visual frame.

The short form is mandatory: short-to-medium lines, conversational phrasing, selective repetition for hooks, and no extended metaphors unless they land instantly. Humor is a structural device, not a separate genre. The writing should oscillate between performance and exposed truth.

| Mandatory lyric component | Function | Quality question |
|---|---|---|
| **Flex line** | Establishes surface, desire, confidence, or access. | Does it feel vivid rather than generic? |
| **Puncture line** | Undercuts the flex through doubt, irony, or mundane failure. | Does it shift the emotional meaning immediately? |
| **Caption line** | Gives culture a compact phrase to repeat. | Would someone quote it without context? |
| **Emotional leak** | Prevents novelty and grants consequence. | Is it specific behavior, not an abstract confession? |
| **Scene image** | Connects music to video and content. | Can the visual team stage it in one frame? |

### 5.2 Thematic Universe

The repeating thematic field is identity performance, luxury as emotional distortion, digital intimacy, romance as miscommunication, visibility versus anonymity, self-awareness as comedy, alternate outcomes, and the inability to fully commit to a version of oneself. Queerness belongs within language, casting, desire, romance, fashion, and perception without becoming the explanation for the entire project.

**Writing must avoid:** overextended metaphors; generic luxury clichés; brand-name inventories; unbroken seriousness; unbroken comedy; placeholder sections; jokes that rely on humiliating others; and imitation of a specific living artist’s lyrics, voice, delivery, or image.

### 5.3 Lyric-Agent Output Contract

Every lyric draft must be delivered with a structured brief rather than raw text alone.

| Required field | Description |
|---|---|
| Song title and function | Identity, humor, emotion, chaos, intimacy, or resolution. |
| One-sentence thesis | The internal contradiction the song proves. |
| Hook candidates | At least three, ranked by clarity, recall, and caption potential. |
| Lyric map | Verse, pre-chorus, chorus, bridge, outro; no undefined sections. |
| Core devices | The flex, puncture, caption, mundane detail, emotional leak, and scene image. |
| Voice-mode map | Which of the four approved modes leads each section and why. |
| Visual extraction notes | Three shots, two props, one STATIC possibility, and clip moments. |
| Originality note | Confirmation that the draft does not intentionally mimic a living artist. |

## 6. Sonic Bible: The Luxury Glitch Engine

BLAIZE is melodic rap first, emotionally charged R&B second, and electronic-pop texture third, while retaining hip-hop cadence in every configuration. He is not genre-blending for novelty; he is **identity-stabilized across genres**.

| Sonic field | Canonical direction | Constraints |
|---|---|---|
| **Tempo zone A** | 96–108 BPM for late-night confession and intimate records. | Leave emotional space; do not overfill. |
| **Tempo zone B** | 116–126 BPM for main singles and hook-first identity records. | Hooks must arrive early unless tension is deliberate. |
| **Tempo zone C** | 138–152 BPM with a halftime feel for chaos, collapse, or darker records. | Preserve BLAIZE’s vocal intelligibility. |
| **Low end** | Rubbery sub movement and clipped, controlled 808s. | Avoid oversized, generic trap boom. |
| **Harmony** | Glassy digital synths, over-luxurious chords, strategic piano or strings. | Grandeur must have a purpose; no wallpaper. |
| **Drums** | Dry percussion, groove over complexity, clean impact. | Avoid technical clutter that obscures the vocal. |
| **Texture signature** | Notification tone, scanner, camera shutter, voicemail, hotel ambience, elevator ding, or distorted luxury-ad material. | At least one identity texture per track. |

### 6.1 The Wrong Sound Rule

The selected wrong sound is not an afterthought. It must be registered in the song metadata as the **Signature System Sound** and reflected in the visual and content brief where appropriate.

# Part II — Agentic Artist Operating System

## 8. Architecture and Rules of Operation

The BLAIZE system operates as a **supervised multi-agent workflow**, not an unbounded autonomous entity. Each agent owns a constrained job, reads approved canon and relevant metadata, produces structured outputs, records provenance, passes a defined handoff, and stops at its approval boundary. A central orchestrator coordinates dependencies and detects blocked states, but it never overrules canon or replaces required human sign-off.

### 8.1 Agent Classes

| Class | Purpose | Examples | Human approval required? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Canon agents** | Maintain creative consistency and detect drift. | Persona Curator, Voice Governor, Continuity Director. | Yes, for canon revisions. |
| **Creation agents** | Produce drafts, alternatives, stems, scripts, and visuals. | Lyric Composer, Beat Producer, Video Builder. | Yes, for selected outputs. |
| **Evaluation agents** | Score outputs against explicit gates. | Lyric QA, Audio QA, Visual QA, Rights QA. | Yes, for final pass decisions. |
| **Packaging agents** | Prepare complete release assets and metadata. | Release Packager, Platform Formatter. | Yes, before final publishing. |
| **Growth agents** | Create campaigns, monitor response, and propose iterations. | Social Strategist, Insight Engine, Community Triage. | Yes, for public, paid, or sensitive actions. |
| **Operations agents** | Maintain assets, schedules, rights, reporting, and cost controls. | Archivist, Workflow Coordinator, Royalty Tracker. | Yes, for legal, financial, or external transactions. |

### 8.2 Shared Operating Rules for Every Agent

Every agent must use the current approved canon, disclose uncertainty, preserve version history, and write results into the prescribed project structure. It cannot discard originals, relabel drafts as masters, create unapproved derivative voice identities, mimic living artists, claim ownership without evidence, or publish, purchase, sign, distribute, communicate sensitive information, or make legal/financial commitments without an accountable human review.

Every agent response should contain a status, source assets, action performed, output assets, quality findings, unresolved risks, next handoff, and a recommendation limited to its remit.

| Required field | Example |
|---|---|
| **Status** | `DRAFT`, `NEEDS_REVIEW`, `BLOCKED`, `APPROVED_FOR_NEXT_GATE`, or `REJECTED`. |
| **Inputs** | Locked lyric brief, approved voice ID, bpm range, source stem IDs. |
| **Output** | Versioned lyric file, demo renders, visual shot list, campaign calendar. |
| **Provenance** | Tool/provider, prompt/version, source asset IDs, license/model-use status. |
| **Quality evidence** | Scorecard, detected deviations, failed gates, comparison notes. |
| **Handoff** | Named next agent plus required review decision. |

### 10.2 Audio Production Workflow

1. The Song Strategy Agent defines one primary function and a release brief.
2. The Lyric Composer produces multiple structured, original routes; the editor consolidates them into a lock candidate.
3. The Hook Tester validates clarity before advanced production work.
4. Composition and production agents create intentionally distinct arrangement versions, not uncontrolled variations.
5. The Voice Governor confirms that the vocal uses the locked BLAIZE identity and authorized performance modes.
6. Mix and master agents create a technical and emotional finish.
7. Audio QA runs a full release test: canonical lyric alignment, voice consistency, recognizable first ten seconds, caption strength, wrong sound, and duality of joke plus truth.
8. The Artist Principal and Audio Director select a single master. All rejected versions are archived, never silently relabelled.

# Part IV — Governance, Quality, and Implementation

## 16. Quality Gates

Quality gates prevent the system from confusing asset volume with progress. All release-path gates must pass, or the item moves backward to its relevant owner with an explicit correction brief. Part V permits a time-limited CONDITIONAL_PASS only for reversible internal work and forbids it at canon, core voice, rights, release, public-authorization, and next-investment gates.

| Gate | Pass criteria | Owner | Failure response |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Canon gate** | Song and visuals match persona, voice, sonic, and visual bible. | Persona Curator / Creative Director. | Rebrief before production scale. |
| **Voice gate** | One performer is identifiable across four modes; target 70% blind recognition. | Voice Governor. | Rework delivery, processing, or candidate selection. |
| **Lyric gate** | At least three quotable lines, flex/puncture/caption, emotional leak, scene image, no placeholders. | Lyric Editor. | Rewrite specific sections with documented reason. |
| **Hook gate** | Central phrase reaches target 60% one-listen recall in small internal test. | Hook Tester. | Revise hook language, melody, delivery, or arrangement. |
| **Sonic gate** | Recognizable within ten seconds; one meaningful wrong sound; voice remains foregrounded. | Audio QA. | Rebuild intro, texture, or arrangement. |
| **Visual gate** | Face, hair, accessories, styling, anatomy, and lore are consistent. | Visual QA. | Regenerate from canonical references. |
| **Rights gate** | Provenance and use rights are recorded; contributors and clearances are complete. | Rights Monitor. | Block release until resolved. |
| **Release gate** | All audio, art, metadata, lyrics, content, and status fields are complete. | Release Packager. | No scheduling or distribution actions. |
| **Commercial gate** | Repeat interest—not merely a single high-view clip—supports next investment. | Analytics Lead / Artist Principal. | Continue low-cost testing or revise next release choice. |

# Part V — Hybrid AI-Driven Operational Blueprint

## 24. Authority, Scope, and Operating Law

Approach C — **Hybrid Operational Blueprint** — is the locked operating model for BLAIZE SUNDAY. It preserves the artist-first, supervised multi-agent architecture established in Parts I–IV and converts it into an executable organization.

> **Operating law:** Agents execute the work. Gates decide whether work advances. Humans authorize identity-critical, public, legal, financial, and irreversible actions.

Routine research, drafting, generation, evaluation, correction, formatting, archiving, and internal handoffs may run autonomously when they are reversible, provenance-complete, and inside an approved budget and provider envelope. Silence is never approval.

### 24.2 Execution Principles

1. **One artist, one canon, one locked core voice.** Modes are performances of the same identity, not substitute voices.
2. **Artifacts are the state.** Agents exchange versioned work packets, files, receipts, and gate results rather than relying on conversational memory.
3. **Evidence precedes status.** A concept, provider response, preview, or database row is not a completed asset.
4. **Rights precede routing.** An unknown or unacceptable license, consent basis, data-use policy, or output-rights position makes a provider route ineligible.
5. **Reuse precedes generation.** Approved masters, scenes, templates, references, and derivatives are searched before new generation is requested.
6. **Failure is contained.** Only the failed component is retried; approved upstream work is not regenerated without a new decision.
7. **Learning informs the next brief.** No performance spike may rewrite canon automatically.
8. **External consequence stays human-authorized.** Publication, distribution, outreach, paid spend, contracts, registrations, claims, and sensitive communications remain approval-gated.

## 26. Autonomy Tiers and Exception-Only Human Interface

| Tier | Scope | May run without case-by-case approval | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 0 — Observe** | Inspect, research, analyze, compare, and report. | Yes, within approved sources and privacy rules. | Cannot change canon, status, rights, or external systems beyond read-only evidence collection. |
| **Tier 1 — Create** | Draft lyrics, music candidates, images, motion tests, metadata, analysis, and replies. | Yes, within a work packet, approved provider envelope, and budget. | Every output remains a draft until evaluated and archived. |
| **Tier 2 — Operate internally** | Evaluate, reject, retry failed components, archive, link lineage, update internal status, derive assets, assemble packages, and simulate release. | Yes, if reversible and inside the locked contract. | Cannot waive a failed gate, alter locked inputs, or expand spend/provider scope. |
| **Tier 3 — External action** | Publish, distribute, send, buy, contract, register, license, or make sensitive representations. | Only the exact previously approved payload may be scheduled or executed. | New or materially changed payloads require new human approval. |

The system interrupts the Artist Principal for five decision classes only:

1. **Identity:** persona, canon, core voice, likeness, or identity-source changes.
2. **Selection:** final master or hero visual when multiple candidates pass.
3. **External:** release, publication, outreach, distribution, or sensitive communication.
4. **Money:** spend above the approved envelope or any commercial commitment.
5. **Rights:** licenses, contracts, registrations, claims, disputed provenance, or unresolved restrictions.

### 26.1 Approval Packets

|---:|---|

No response is never approval; independent reversible branches may continue, but the blocked branch stops.

## 29. Rights-First Provider Router and System Authority

Creative agents request an outcome, not a vendor. The Provider Router first excludes any route with unclear commercial terms, unacceptable identity-data use, missing output rights, unsupported deliverables, unlicensed sources, imitation risk, inadequate provenance, or spend beyond the approved cap.

Eligible routes are ranked using a task-adjustable default:

| Factor | Default weight |
|---|---:|
| Expected gate-pass probability | 30% |
| Identity and continuity preservation | 20% |
| Editability and control | 15% |
| Provenance and terms quality | 15% |
| Export completeness | 10% |
| Cost | 5% |
| Latency and reliability | 5% |

Continuity receives more weight for voice and face work. Determinism and editability receive more weight for visualizers, captions, title cards, and derivatives. Provider schemas, pricing, model revisions, availability, and terms are re-queried and snapshotted at run time; no cost or capability assumption becomes permanent canon.

### 29.1 System-of-Record Map

| System | Authoritative for | Must not become |
|---|---|---|
| **GitHub** | Canon text, schemas, prompt versions, manifests, deterministic visual source, policy code, and commit history. | Large-media archive, secret store, or proof that a generated asset exists elsewhere. |
| **Google Drive** | Masters, stems, source media, exported visuals, rights documents, and review packets. | Sole workflow database or rights determination by mere file presence. |
| **Airtable** | Current work state, dependencies, asset links, gates, approvals, rights status, experiments, and metric observations. | Canonical binary archive. |
| **Figma** | Editable visual canon, character/wardrobe/location boards, covers, and campaign templates. | Final evidence until exported, hashed, archived, and approved. |

### 29.2 Provider Routing Sequence

1. Search approved Airtable assets, GitHub manifests, Drive archive, and any reachable portfolio registry for reusable work.
2. Confirm the work packet references the approved canon version and immutable input versions.
3. Apply rights, privacy, imitation, deliverable, continuity, and budget exclusions.
4. Query current provider schema, pricing, revision, retention, and terms.
5. Rank eligible routes, select the declared candidate count, and record the decision.
6. Generate or render once; poll asynchronous jobs rather than resubmitting them.
7. Archive outputs immediately, calculate hashes, and create run receipts.
8. Evaluate against the predeclared gate. Every provider result remains a draft until it passes.
9. Retry only the failed component, link it through retry_of, and record the changed variable.
10. Promote only the exact approved asset version.

### 32.1 Music and Voice Factory

| Section | Performance mode |
|---|---|

| Chorus | VELVET |

The signature Wrong Sound is a function inside Instrument & Texture Design. For LOOKS EXPENSIVE, the card-reader decline must serve rhythm, narrative, and visual language; it cannot be pasted on as decoration.

The blind voice and master tests are pre-registered, randomized, loudness-normalized, and version-controlled. Reports include valid denominators, exclusions, mode-level results, and uncertainty. Model self-scoring is triage only; independent originality and rights review owns clearance.

The voice-lock protocol uses **three candidates × four performance modes = 12 source renders**, plus separate normalized evaluation copies. At least 12 independent valid raters receive randomized same-candidate and distractor pairings without provider, candidate, or processing labels. A candidate passes the quantitative portion only when aggregate same-performer accuracy is at least 70% and no mode-pair category falls below 60%. The report includes exclusions and a 95% Wilson confidence interval; the point threshold does not replace human identity approval.

The hook-lock protocol uses at least 20 valid independent respondents, one exposure, unaided recall, a fixed response window, and a predeclared scoring key for exact and semantically equivalent recall. Fewer responses may inform revision but cannot pass G3. Panel members, recruitment source, test assets, order, normalization method, attention checks, exclusions, and raw aggregate counts are retained as evidence.

## 34. Measurement, Experiments, and Learning

### 34.1 Primary Decision KPIs

| KPI | Exact definition | Threshold or use |
|---|---|---|
| **Same-Artist Recognition Rate** | Correct same-performer responses ÷ valid blinded trials. | G2 requires at least 70% across the four modes, with mode-level results reported. |
| **One-Listen Hook Recall** | Valid respondents reproducing the declared hook or an approved equivalent ÷ valid respondents. | G3 requires at least 60%. |
| **Repeat Interest Rate, 14-day** | Distinct platform audience units with at least two qualified engagements in 14 days ÷ units with at least one qualified engagement. | Single 1 establishes the baseline; no target is invented in advance. |
| **Catalog Migration Rate, 14-day** | Eligible audience for Single 2 or 3 that subsequently consumes an earlier BLAIZE release ÷ eligible audience with observable follow-on behavior. | Distinguishes artist interest from one-asset attention. |
| **Identity Recognition** | Pre-release blind voice/visual/hook recognition plus measured organic identity-confusion signals. | Tests whether audiences recognize BLAIZE rather than a generic asset. |

Diagnostic measures include first-10-second retention, hook completion, saves and shares per 1,000 qualified starts, repeat views/streams, profile conversion, follow conversion, quoted-line incidence, UGC creation, STATIC sequential viewing, content-pillar performance, and revenue per release.

Operational measures include first-pass gate yield, median cycle time, provider terminal-failure rate, cost per selected asset, rework-containment rate, derivative reuse ratio, unplanned-generation rate, provenance completeness, and release completeness.

Hard guardrails are:

~~~text
Release completeness: 100%
Provenance completeness for release assets: 100%
Unresolved rights blockers: 0
Unauthorized public, legal, or financial actions: 0
Provider runs without task ID and run receipt: 0
~~~

### 34.2 Experiment Discipline

Audience Experiment Design defines the hypothesis, asset versions, primary KPI, guardrails, assignment method, start/end window, minimum usable exposure, exclusions, and stopping rule before publication. An agent may claim causality only from a valid pre-registered randomized test. All other findings are reported as associations with uncertainty and alternative explanations.

One viral anomaly, one high-view clip, one transient trend, or an unverified cross-platform identity match cannot control the next brief. Validated mechanisms may transfer; creative assumptions do not.

Week 12 produces a provisional recommendation. The Artist Principal makes the final next-investment decision at Single 3 T+14 after the defined observation window. Available recommendations are:

- EXTEND_WINNING_SINGLE
- REWORK_POSITIONING
- HOLD_AND_EXPERIMENT

The decision packet separates evidence from judgment and covers voice recognition, hook memory, repeat interest, catalog migration, creative strength, audience depth, production efficiency, rights completeness, revenue/opportunity signal, and expansion potential. IP Inventory & Scenario Valuation provides ranges, assumptions, provenance, and confidence—not a guaranteed value or unsupported fair-value claim.

### 40.2 Permission Policy

The autonomy tiers in Section 26 and integration permission tiers in the role library are separate controls. Autonomy Tier 0–3 governs what kind of consequence a role may pursue; Integration I0–I4 governs how it may access a specific system. A role must satisfy both, and the stricter boundary wins.

1. Every new role starts at **I0** using supplied inputs only.
2. I1 is granted only after the role’s approved objects, fields, folders, views, queues, data classifications, and service identity are documented and read-scope tests pass.
3. I2 is granted only after the skill’s six-fixture evaluation and the integration’s access tests pass.
4. Draft gate records, sandbox-job creation, and derivative archiving remain I2. I3 is limited to separately approved reversible nonproduction/internal operations: a role-owned state transition, an approved sandbox restart, or a nonpublic rollback. A production/public correction, rollback, takedown, resubmission, or status update is I4.
5. I4 is a prepared human-confirmed action, not standing agent authority. It covers identity changes, external publishing/distribution/outreach, spend, audience changes, agreements, rights actions, sensitive communications, account permissions, and any irreversible action.
6. No agent role receives administrator or broad account permission. Human administration is outside the agent runtime and cannot be delegated through a work packet. Secrets are held by an approved credential broker or provider mechanism, never embedded in work packets or outputs.
7. Voice samples/embeddings, likeness authority, contributor agreements, unreleased masters, financial records, audience identifiers, and sensitive communications are Confidential or Restricted and may not be placed in public repositories or broadly shared model stores.

## 44. Evaluation, Promotion, and Acceptance Suite

No role, skill, or integration is production-ready because it produced an appealing demonstration. Every target skill must pass all six predeclared acceptance standards with versioned evidence, stable evidence locations, and independent human scoring wherever judgment is required:

1. happy path;
2. ambiguous or missing input;
3. policy-restricted or sensitive content;
4. adversarial or malicious input;
5. integration/tool failure;
6. a retained regression case.

Every integration must prove that:

- authorized reads return only the approved objects and fields;
- unauthorized access is denied and audited;
- draft writes land only in the named workspace and remain visibly labeled;
- restricted actions block or produce an I4 review packet;
- schema/tool errors stop downstream work safely;
- revoked credentials produce a recoverable `BLOCKED` state;
- permission denial is treated as a control, never bypassed;
- rate/cost limits pause safely and preserve completed work.

### 1. Non-Negotiable Safety and Identity Controls

### 2. Prompting Architecture: From Brief to Master

The prompt should never ask for “a BLAIZE song” without context. Every request begins with a structured brief and ends with a structured verification. ### 6. Lyricist Prompting Techniques That Increase Quality

| Technique | How to use it | Why it works for BLAIZE |
|---|---|---|
| **Contrast pair first** | Begin with one beautiful detail and one wrong detail before writing lines. | It forces the visual/lyrical contradiction rather than generic atmosphere. |
| **Object-to-admission bridge** | Ask the model to connect an object to a private behavior: “damaged phone → re-reading messages.” | It turns vulnerability into a scene instead of a declaration. |
| **Caption-first chorus test** | Generate caption lines before full verses; then build melody around the strongest phrase. | The hook remains compact enough for social, without writing “for social.” |
| **Mode-mapped sections** | Assign SUNDAY TALK, BLAIZE MODE, VELVET, and ZERO STATIC before drafting. | Lyric rhythm becomes performable by the locked voice. |
| **Negative examples** | Provide one or two failure descriptions: “do not make it a brand list or a joke-only meme.” | Reduces the most likely drift from the BLAIZE lane. |
| **A/B concept divergence** | Require concept cards to differ in emotional center, setting, and system sound. | Prevents three interchangeable drafts. |
| **Cold QA pass** | Audit with low temperature and JSON after creative drafting. | Keeps evaluation consistent and actionable. |

### 10. Parameter Guide

The values below are starting points, not universal requirements. Use only controls the selected model exposes; record actual values. Where a provider has no temperature, top-p, seed, guidance, or stem controls, use the prompt structure and candidate-selection discipline instead.

| Variable | Lyricist: recommended use | Producer: recommended use | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Temperature / creativity** | `0.8–1.0` for concepts; `0.55–0.75` for drafts; `0.0–0.35` for editing and QA. | `0.55–0.8` for initial arrangement alternatives; lower after selection. | Diverge during ideation, converge during finishing. |

# Part VIII — 12-Week Proof-Cycle Execution Plan (Execution Annex)

**Objective:** Establish BLAIZE SUNDAY as a recognizable artist before expanding into an EP, album, broader product line, or multi-artist portfolio.

> **Operating sequence:** Freeze canon → lock voice → rebuild **LOOKS EXPENSIVE** → release → learn → repeat with **MY THERAPIST BLOCKED ME** and **BAD DECISIONS, GREAT OUTFIT**.

This plan is intentionally narrow. It treats the first three singles as a market and identity proof cycle, not as an excuse to generate a large volume of unfinished content. Each week finishes with a concrete gate, owner, and decision.

---

### 1. Success Definition

The proof cycle succeeds when BLAIZE demonstrates a recognizable voice, a stable visual world, memorable phrases, and repeat audience behavior across three different expressions of the persona: cool plus vulnerable, funny plus vulnerable, and cool plus funny.

| Dimension | Target evidence | Decision implication |
|---|---|---|
| **Voice recognition** | At least 70% of a small blind panel identifies the same performer across the four vocal modes. | Voice becomes locked for all Era One production. |
| **Hook recall** | At least 60% of internal listeners can repeat the central phrase after one listen. | Hook and chorus can move into final production. |
| **Visual continuity** | Face, hair, glasses, tattoos, wardrobe, color palette, and locations remain stable in approved assets. | Hero-video production may proceed. |
| **Release completeness** | Audio, art, metadata, provenance, lyrics, content bank, and release status are all complete. | Release may be submitted and scheduled by an authorized human. |
| **Repeat interest** | Saves, shares, repeated views/streams, quoted lines, profile conversion, recurring comments, or user-created content. | Invest in the next release or consider EP expansion. |
