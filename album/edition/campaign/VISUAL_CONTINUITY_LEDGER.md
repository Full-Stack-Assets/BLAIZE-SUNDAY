# BLAIZE SUNDAY — Era One Visual Continuity Ledger

**State:** `APPROVED_PRODUCTION_BRIEF / FINAL ASSETS REQUIRE HUMAN AUTHORITY`  
**Era:** LOOKS EXPENSIVE, FEELS WEIRD  
**Creative law:** beautiful surface + one deliberately wrong/corrupted detail.

## Identity anchors

Every generated BLAIZE candidate must preserve the same recognizable person across still and motion work:

- black rectangular glasses;
- large black ear gauges;
- prominent eye tattoo centered on the throat/neck;
- extensive arm and hand tattoo coverage;
- slim athletic build and consistent body proportions;
- calm, direct, slightly detached expression;
- luxury-meets-street styling;
- jewelry/chains/watch/grills when contextually appropriate;
- consistent age presentation, facial proportions and skin tone;
- no unexplained face, hair, tattoo or anatomy drift.

## Current Human Authority wardrobe constraint

New campaign generations must **exclude fur coats and fur outerwear**, even where earlier Era One documents used fur as a possible texture. This is a current task-scoped superseding visual constraint.

For the current candidate campaign, each look should include a **color-coordinated Chicago Bulls snapback and Goyard crossbody bag** as requested by Human Authority. These branded elements remain `CANDIDATE` only until the rights/trademark decision is made. Final release artwork can be de-branded without changing composition or wardrobe silhouette.

## Camera continuity

- 35–50mm cinematic realism as the default language;
- close physical proximity rather than distant spectacle;
- slow push-ins, lateral tracking, locked sterile frames and direct-flash inserts;
- purposeful negative space for title/metadata adaptations;
- skin and tattoos remain photorealistic, not airbrushed into plastic texture;
- no random luxury props without narrative function;
- no generic cyberpunk/neon treatment replacing the mundane-system premise.

## Three campaign modes

### Luxury Noir

Tracks: LOOKS EXPENSIVE, DELETE AFTER LISTENING, 2:17 AM, ROOM SERVICE FOR ONE.

Use dark hotel/apartment interiors, black leather, polished stone, chrome, liquid amber, bone/ivory and bruised violet. Isolation matters more than spectacle. No new fur.

### Beautiful but Wrong

Tracks: MY THERAPIST BLOCKED ME, NO SIGNAL, WRONG FLOOR.

Use therapy rooms, parking decks, gas stations, convenience spaces, sterile hallways, elevators and vending areas. Styling remains immaculate while infrastructure or social context quietly malfunctions.

### Flash / Motion / Flex

Tracks: BAD DECISIONS, GREAT OUTFIT; PRETTY BOY PROBLEMS; PARALLEL YOU.

Use cars, nightlife, performance, editorial streetwear, hard flash, mirrored surfaces, movement and attention. The image must still contain a vulnerability/error cue rather than pure flex photography.

## Reject conditions

A still or video frame is rejected if any of these occur:

1. face no longer reads as the same person;
2. throat-eye tattoo is absent, relocated or materially redesigned without a purposeful crop;
3. glasses/ear gauges disappear without an intentional story reason;
4. hands/tattoos mutate visibly;
5. physique/age changes materially;
6. clothing contains fur in a new generation;
7. branded accessories become dominant product-placement shots;
8. the image contains luxury but no wrong/corrupted detail;
9. visual mode conflicts with the assigned song without documented rationale;
10. anatomy, reflections or mirrored duplicates are visibly broken rather than intentionally surreal.
