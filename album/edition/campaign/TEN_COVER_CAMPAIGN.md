The ten covers are one campaign, not ten unrelated generations. All use cinematic photorealism, restrained typography-safe composition, the same BLAIZE identity anchors, and the Era One law: **beautiful surface + one wrong thing**.

## 01 — LOOKS EXPENSIVE

**ID:** `ART-CAND-01-LOOKS-EXPENSIVE`  
**Mode:** Luxury Noir  
**Scene:** immaculate five-star hotel checkout desk at night. BLAIZE stands in tailored black/red street-luxury layers, matching black Chicago Bulls snapback and black/red Goyard crossbody. He holds a metal room key and a payment card while a small card reader glows red. The hotel is expensive and perfectly lit; the wrong detail is the declined transaction and a subtly malfunctioning chandelier segment.  
**Perspective:** low three-quarter full-body frame, 40mm, polished stone reflections, negative space upper-left.  
**Emotional cue:** public certainty, private irritation.  
**Avoid:** fashion-ad emptiness, comedy-face reaction, logo closeup.

## 02 — MY THERAPIST BLOCKED ME

## Campaign output contract

For every selected candidate after Human Authority art approval:

1. preserve the approved composition as the primary visual master;
2. export a 3000×3000 square cover without crop-induced identity loss;
3. create a 9:16 vertical adaptation by extending/reframing the environment rather than stretching the subject;
4. create a 16:9 thumbnail with title-safe negative space;
5. record provider/model, source references, generation ID, timestamp and final SHA-256;
6. run continuity QA against face, throat tattoo, glasses, gauges, hands/tattoos, build and wardrobe;
7. apply de-branding if Bulls/Goyard clearance is not affirmatively approved for commercial publication.
