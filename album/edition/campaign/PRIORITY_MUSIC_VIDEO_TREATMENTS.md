# 01 — LOOKS EXPENSIVE

## Thesis

BLAIZE moves through a five-star hotel as if every system belongs to him. The physical world agrees until tiny operational systems keep rejecting him. The video never turns him into a clown; the environment fails quietly around a person who refuses to look rattled.

## Visual mode

Luxury Noir, no fur. Black/red/bone styling with matching Bulls snapback and Goyard crossbody in candidate footage; prepare a de-branded wardrobe pass for release if trademarks remain uncleared.

## 2:30–3:30 scene architecture

**00:00–00:12 — Arrival / identity proof**  
Locked 40mm shot in hotel corridor. BLAIZE walks toward camera, shoulders relaxed, glasses on, bag crossbody, tattoos visible. A room-service employee passes without reaction. Elevator bell lands just before the first vocal entrance.

**00:12–00:35 — The first malfunction**  
At the room door, key card flashes red. BLAIZE looks at the card, not the camera. Cut to hotel check-in desk. The desk itself is immaculate; the reader fails again. The failure is small enough that his pose survives.

**00:35–01:00 — Chorus / public image**  
Performance coverage in polished lobby and glass elevator. Slow circular camera move, direct eye contact, high-detail wardrobe. On the title phrase, a card-reader red pulse briefly becomes a practical lighting accent.

**01:00–01:28 — Verse 2 / locked out**  
Silk-shirt or structured-jacket look in quiet corridor, socks/shoes detail against stone floor, dead hotel key in hand. No slapstick. Security camera watches from the ceiling. BLAIZE gives it the same nod he would give a person.

**01:28–01:50 — Chorus 2 / certainty loop**  
Repeat lobby choreography with one deliberate change: every passing screen says approved except his transaction terminal. Use hard flash inserts, no constant glitch.

**01:50–02:15 — ZERO STATIC bridge**  
Private hotel room finally opens. No performance blocking. Jacket off, quiet bathroom mirror, flat room tone, one static camera. His face remains recognizable without the full styling armor. No branded objects in hero frame.

**02:15–end — Final chorus / approved too late**  
Return to front desk. Reader finally glows green after he has already turned away. Last image: receipt prints into an empty foreground while BLAIZE walks toward elevator. Cut before explanation.

# 02 — MY THERAPIST BLOCKED ME

## Thesis

A therapy appointment is staged as an immaculate subscription/customer-service workflow. The comedy comes from institutional language behaving with absurd precision while BLAIZE slowly runs out of material to perform for.

## Visual mode

Beautiful but Wrong. Cream/brown/pink palette, soft medical white, polished waiting-room wood and steel. No fur.

## 2:30–3:30 scene architecture

**00:00–00:15 — Hold state**  
BLAIZE sits alone in an upscale therapy waiting room. Check-in tablet shows a generic loading state. A tasteful hold-phone speaker plays silently in-frame; no copyrighted interface branding.

**00:15–00:42 — Verse 1 / administrative comedy**  
Cut between check-in desk, massage-table-like absurdity, calendar reminders and BLAIZE composing/deleting text on his phone. Keep performance dry and close. No exaggerated reaction shots.

**00:42–01:05 — Chorus / session terminated**  
Waiting-room doors open to reveal an empty therapy room. BLAIZE performs toward the empty chair. A small status light changes from amber to red during the title line.

**01:05–01:32 — Verse 2 / hold music**  
Car interior or parking-garage vehicle bay. He harmonizes with a generic original hold-music motif, but the camera stays serious. Cut back to pristine apartment where he changes sheets, deletes a contact, then visibly remembers the number anyway.

**01:32–01:58 — Chorus 2**  
Therapy room now contains two chairs but no therapist. Camera slowly moves closer. The interface behind him keeps offering generic wellness options while the lyric becomes more specific.

**01:58–02:20 — ZERO STATIC bridge**  
Apartment at night. Refrigerator hum, no score-like visual effects, no audience. BLAIZE stands in the kitchen in bad fluorescent light, styling deliberately less protected but identity unchanged.

**02:20–end — Final chorus / boundary accepted**  
Back to waiting room. He closes the tablet, leaves without resolving whether the session failed or succeeded. The status light turns green after the elevator doors close.
