# Native-Stem Master Replacement Map

The present edition uses catalog state `CURATED_REFERENCE_MASTER`. The future designation `NATIVE_STEM_MASTER` is reserved for a rebuild whose source session preserves independently created/recorded synchronized native components before final mixdown.

A native replacement must preserve, at minimum, dry lead vocal, backing/harmony vocals, doubles/ad-libs, spoken material, vocal FX returns, drums, bass, melodic/instrumental material, effects/transitions, full instrumental, pre-master full mix, and approved master.

A native replacement supersedes only the audio object and its audio-specific provenance/QC. Track IDs, lyrics, approved artwork, campaign assets, video treatments, credits that remain accurate, and release analytics continue under the same namespace. Replacement requires a new Human Authority master approval receipt and may not inherit approval merely because the prior derived edition was approved.
